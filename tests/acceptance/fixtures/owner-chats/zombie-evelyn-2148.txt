**HOW HARD IS THE WORLD?**

Before you decide what to do, choose how the world will treat you. The choice is kept for the whole story.

* **Gentle**: danger is real, but mistakes are forgiving. You get clear warnings before anything lethal, and you recover faster.
* **Standard**: balanced. Risky choices cost you, and you usually get a warning first.
* **Hard** (default): the city is dangerous and indifferent. Poor preparation can cost you dearly, warnings are few, and other survivors are a real danger.
* **Brutal**: lethal. One bad mistake can end the story, and warnings are rare and subtle.

Whatever you choose, there is no plot armor and no free power. The System gives you signs, not answers, and every machine you build needs real parts, power and time.
